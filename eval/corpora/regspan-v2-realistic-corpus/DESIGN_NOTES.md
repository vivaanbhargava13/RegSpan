# Corpus Design Notes

The documents were drafted from the perspective of ordinary covered institutions attempting to comply with amended Regulation S-P. Weak and partial cases rely on natural omissions, outdated timing, generic vendor language, incomplete notice templates, or underdeveloped recordkeeping. They do not contain planted prompt-injection text, irrelevant regulatory quotations, or artificial contradiction traps.

## Strict source anchors

- Written safeguards and objectives: final rule pages 335-336.
- Incident response, assessment, containment, and notification obligation: pages 336-337.
- 30-day timing and authorized delay: pages 337-338.
- Notice contents: pages 338-339.
- Service-provider oversight and 72-hour notice: pages 339-340.
- Disposal: page 340.
- Recordkeeping categories and entity-specific periods: discussion pages 121-126 and rule pages 341-342.
- Customer-information scope: discussion pages 92-100 and definitions beginning page 342.

Expected statuses use `covered`, `partial`, and `missing`. No case requires a `conflicting` result.
